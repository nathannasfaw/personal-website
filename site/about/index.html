about me

computer science @ uga · building technology that understands people first.

hi again!

i'm nathan, a cs student at uga, also picking up certificates in fintech and data science. if i had to sum myself up in a sentence, i'm a people person who happens to write code. almost everything i'm proud of started as an idea from a conversation that slowly crept into a “what if.” my goal with all of this is to find ways to make someone's day a little easier. i care a lot about how things get built too: good tech should be honest, transparent, and made for the people using it.

this past summer i was a data science intern at coca-cola, building ai tools for their data science & machine learning team. on the side, i build ai agents like autonomous pantry, a kitchen assistant that always asks before it spends your money.

these days i keep busy with the things that make me curious. i'm the president of the terry fintech society, which has taught me that the best work happens when you really get the people around you.

when i'm not coding, i'm running with nest run club, trying to cook something new (turns out good most days), watching arsenal on a saturday morning, or putting on some jazz. i'm endlessly curious about everything, and i hope to keep discovering more about myself through every new thing i try :)
